01 — Brand idea

Open frame + mission core

The primary mark combines an open frame with a blue core node. The frame is the physical case and sovereign boundary — inspectable, extensible, modular. The opening means capabilities can attach and detach; the system is not sealed. The blue node is the operator-controlled mission core.

It must not read as a shield, surveillance eye, weapon, or military unit. Selected direction: Concept 01 mark + Concept 05 blue-hyphen wordmark. No further mark exploration.

02 — Construction

Geometry

Drawn on a 24-unit grid. Stroke mass is a consistent 2.5u with round joins and caps. Opening sits at the top-right and is softly deformed — not a hard rectangular bite. Only the core node is a sharp 4×4u square centered at (12, 12).

  • Frame corners are soft/rounded — not a hard square enclosure
  • Opening terminals are slightly organic (round caps + gentle inward curve)
  • Blue core stays perfectly sharp — the only right angles in the mark
  • Flat solids / strokes — no gradients, shadows, bevels, or 3D
  • Opening is deliberate and permanent — do not close the frame

08 — Clear space & minima

One stroke of air, always

Clear space equals one stroke width (2.5u) on all sides of the outer frame. Nothing enters the opening or covers the core node.

11 — Incorrect usage

Never

✕ CLOSED FRAME

Close the opening

The opening is the extensibility signal

✕ HARD CUT

Square-cut opening

Opening ends stay soft and slightly deformed

✕ ROTATED

Rotate 90°

Keep upright; opening stays top-right

✕ STRETCHED

Distort proportions

Lock the 24u square

✕ EFFECTS

Glow / 3D / gradient

Flat solids only, forever
